Growing Moringa in Trinidad – My Experience

I have been growing moringa for years in Trinidad and currently have two mature trees in my backyard, both producing seed pods, flowers, and an abundance of fresh leaves year round. I also keep several plants in containers for easy access closer to my kitchen.

Here is what I have learned from years of growing it in a tropical climate:

What works:

  • Moringa germinates easily and quickly in Trinidad’s heat, one of the fastest germinating trees I have grown
  • It grows aggressively, my trees are large and productive with minimal intervention
  • Fresh seed pods, flowers, and leaves are available almost continuously
  • I have dried and powdered my own leaves for personal use

What to watch for:

  • Moringa does not tolerate soggy soil, drainage is essential even in containers
  • Leaf miners can be an issue, small tunnels appear in the leaves but are manageable
  • Some yellowing of older leaves is normal and not a sign of disease
  • Container plants need more frequent watering but give you more control over soil conditions

Drying and powdering fresh leaves takes time and equipment. The capsules are a convenient option for anyone who prefers moringa leaf powder without growing, drying, and measuring it themselves.
